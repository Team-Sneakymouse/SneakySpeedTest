Send us the useful bits

Your measurements can help explain the slow downloads.

What we collect

  • Download speeds, timings, byte counts, test order, and any errors.
  • Available Cloudflare edge IDs, cache status, and file response headers.
  • Browser information, language, and whether the tab was hidden during the test.
  • Your country and network ASN when Cloudflare provides them.
  • Your Minecraft username, only if you enter it.

What we don't store

  • Your raw IP address.
  • The downloaded resourcepack contents.
  • Cookies, browsing history, or Minecraft account credentials.

The application uses no analytics or tracking cookies. Cloudflare and the hosting infrastructure process your connection as part of serving the site.

This is a temporary investigation. The operator plans to remove the deployment and delete its results in about two weeks. There is no automatic expiry.

Review your report

The server adds its submission timestamp and the country/ASN from your connection when you submit. Unknown values stay empty.
